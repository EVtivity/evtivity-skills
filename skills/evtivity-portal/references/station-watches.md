# Station Watch

Get notified when a busy or offline station has a free connector.

## Overview

Station Watch notifies you when a station frees up. When all of a station's connectors are in use, or the station is offline, you can watch it. As soon as a connector becomes available, EVtivity sends you a notification through your configured channels (email, SMS, or push). The Watching page is reached from the eye icon in the portal header, between the favorites star and the notification bell.

![Watching page](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/portal/station-watches.png)

## Watching a Station

To watch a station:

1. Open the Station Detail page for any charging station.
2. If no connector is currently available, a **Notify me when free** button appears above Report Issue.
3. Tap **Notify me when free**. The button changes to **Remove from Watching** to confirm. Tap it again to stop watching.

The **Notify me when free** button is shown only when the station has no available connector. If a connector is already free, the button is hidden because there is nothing to wait for and you can start charging right away.

## Getting Notified

When a connector at a watched station becomes available, you receive a notification. Delivery follows your notification preferences, the same ones used for every other driver alert. The watch is one-shot: it clears itself the moment it notifies you. If the station fills up again, open it and tap **Notify me when free** to start a new watch.

## Watching Page

The eye icon in the portal header opens the Watching page and shows a count badge when you have active watches. Each card lists:

- **Station name** - the operator-assigned station identifier
- **Site name** - the site where the station is located
- **Address** - full street address of the site
- **Availability** - how many connectors are currently available
- **Status indicator** - a green dot when the station is online, a gray dot when offline

Tap the trash icon on a card to stop watching that station.

## Notes

- A watch expires automatically after 24 hours if the station does not free up.
- You can watch up to 25 stations at the same time.
- Starting a charging session at a station you are watching clears that watch automatically.
- Watches are tied to your driver account and are independent of favorites.
- Station Watch is always available to authenticated drivers; the operator can still disable the notification globally from the Notifications settings.
