# Favorites

Save and manage favorite charging stations for quick access.

## Overview

Drivers can save favorite stations for quick access. The Favorites page is accessible from the star icon in the portal header, located to the left of the notification bell.

![Favorites page](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/portal/favorites.png)

## Adding a Favorite

To add a station to your favorites:

1. Open the Station Detail page for any charging station.
2. Tap the star icon next to the station ID, or tap the **Add to Favorites** button.
3. The star icon fills in to confirm the station was saved.

Favorited stations show a filled star icon on the Station Detail page. Stations that have not been favorited show an outlined star.

## Favorites Page

The Favorites page lists all saved stations as cards. Each card displays the following information:

- **Station name** - the operator-assigned station identifier
- **Site name** - the site where the station is located
- **Address** - full street address of the site
- **EVSE count** - total number of connectors at the station
- **Available count** - how many connectors are currently available for charging
- **Connector types** - the connector standards supported (CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2, etc.)

### Status Indicators

Each station card shows an online or offline status indicator:

- **Green dot** - the station is online and communicating with the CSMS
- **Gray dot** - the station is offline or not responding

These indicators update each time you open the Favorites page.

## Removing a Favorite

To remove a station from your favorites:

1. Open the Favorites page from the star icon in the portal header.
2. Tap the trash icon on the station card you want to remove.
3. The station is removed from the list immediately.

You can also unfavorite a station by tapping the filled star icon on the Station Detail page.

## Notes

- Favorites are tied to your driver account and persist across devices.
- The Favorites page is only available to authenticated drivers.
- If a station is taken offline or removed by the operator, it still appears in your favorites with an offline indicator.
