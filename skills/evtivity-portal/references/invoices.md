# Invoices

View your invoices and credit notes, including the monthly invoices your fleet addresses to you, and download each as a PDF.

## Overview

The Invoices page lists the invoices and credit notes the operator issued to you. Open it from the Account page: tap **Invoices**.

It shows two kinds of invoices:

- **Member invoices**: when your fleet bills your charging on account and invoices each member, you receive one invoice per month for your own sessions. These show "Via" and the name of your fleet.
- **Driver invoices**: invoices the operator issued to you for sessions you paid yourself.

A fleet invoice addressed to the fleet itself never shows here. It goes to the fleet, not to you.

## Invoice List

Each row shows:

- **Invoice** or **Credit Note** and its number
- The status: **Issued**, **Paid**, or **Credited**
- For a member invoice, the fleet it comes through
- The issue date and the total

The newest invoices come first. When you have many invoices, use the page buttons at the bottom. With no invoices, the page shows "No invoices yet."

Tap a row to open the invoice.

## Invoice Detail

The detail page shows:

- **Period**: the month the invoice bills
- **Issued**, **Due**, and **Paid** dates
- **Bill To**: the name, address, and email printed on the invoice
- **Items**: the lines of the invoice, then the **Net** amount, the tax, and the **Total**
- For a credit note, the invoice it credits and the **Reason**. A credited invoice names the credit note that cancels it.

Tap **Download PDF** to save the invoice as a PDF. It is the same PDF you received by email.

## How You Receive a Member Invoice

When your fleet issues your member invoice, you receive it by email with the PDF attached and as a push notification. A credit note or a payment reminder for an overdue invoice reaches you the same way. These messages are always sent, whatever your notification preferences. They are never sent by SMS.

The bill-to block of a member invoice uses your billing address. Add it on the **Personal Info** page. See [Billing Address](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/portal/account#billing-address). Without an address, the invoice shows your name and email.

## Notes

- Your sessions on a member invoice show "Billed to you via" and the fleet name, with a link to the invoice. See [Activity and Sessions](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/portal/sessions#session-detail-receipt).
- You pay a member invoice as your operator or fleet tells you, for example by bank transfer. The portal does not take card payments for invoices.
- The mobile app shows the same invoices. See [Payments](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/payments#invoices).
