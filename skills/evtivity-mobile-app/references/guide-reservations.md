# Reservations

Reserve a connector now or for later, view your reservations, and cancel one.

## Overview

A reservation holds a connector so it is free when you arrive. The Reserve tab lists your upcoming and past reservations and lets you book a new one. Reservations are available only if your operator has turned them on.

![Reservations list](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/reservations.png)

## Make a reservation

![New reservation form](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/reservation-new.png)

1. Tap the **Reserve** tab, then **New Reservation**.
2. Search for a station by name or address and select it.
3. Choose **Now** to start right away, or **Schedule** to pick a date and time within the next seven days.
4. Choose a duration: 30, 60, or 120 minutes.
5. Tap **Confirm**.

The reservation appears under **Upcoming**.

## View your reservations

The Reserve tab has two tabs of its own:

- **Upcoming** - reservations that are scheduled or active now.
- **Past** - reservations that expired, were cancelled, or became a session.

Tap any reservation to see its full detail: the station, the start and expiry times, the connector, and its status. Pull down to refresh the list.

## Start charging from a reservation

When your reservation is active, go to the station and start as usual. The connector is already held for you. If the reservation converts into a charging session, its detail links to that session.

## Cancel a reservation

1. Open the reservation from the Upcoming list, or swipe it.
2. Tap **Cancel Reservation**.
3. Confirm with **Cancel Reservation**.

> **Warning:**
>
> Some operators charge a fee if you cancel close to the start time or let a reservation expire unused. If a fee applies, the app warns you before you confirm.
