# Account and Settings

Manage your profile, security, notification channels, language, units, and legal information.

## Overview

The Account tab is the hub for everything about you: your profile, security, payment methods, RFID cards, vehicles, favorites, support, and app information. It shows your avatar, name, email, and the date you joined.

![Account hub](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/account.png)

## Avatar

Your avatar shows at the top of the Account hub. Without one, your initials show. Tap it to open the **Avatar** screen. You have one avatar at a time, and the driver portal and the operator see the same one.

- **Create** - pick the hair or headwear, glasses, eyebrows, eyes, mouth, facial hair, clothes, colors, background and direction, or tap **Random**. Hair or headwear, glasses and facial hair also offer **None**. Tap **Save** to keep it.
- **Upload** - tap **Choose Image** and pick a photo from your library. Crop it to a square in the picker. The image must be a JPEG, PNG or WebP smaller than 1 MB. It is saved as soon as it uploads, scaled to at most 512 x 512 pixels. **Upload** shows only when the operator has set up file storage.
- **Remove Avatar** - deletes an uploaded image and shows your initials again.

An operator with the permission can also set or remove your avatar in the CSMS.

## Personal info

![Personal info screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/personal.png)

Tap **Personal Info** to edit your first and last name and your phone number. Your email is shown but cannot be changed here. When your account uses two-factor authentication, the app tells you to change the phone number in the driver portal, where you confirm the change. Below that, set your preferences:

- **Language** - the app updates immediately when you change it.
- **Distance unit** - miles or kilometers.
- **Timezone** - used for session and statement times.
- **Show prices** - **Including tax (gross)**, **Excluding tax (net)**, or the operator default. It sets whether charger prices and session costs are shown with or without tax. You pay the same amount either way. The choice is saved on your account, so the driver portal uses it too.

Save to apply your changes.

## Invoices and billing address

Tap **Invoices** to see your invoices and credit notes and share their PDFs. When your fleet invoices you for your sessions, add your billing address so it prints on your invoices. See [Payments](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/payments#invoices).

## Security

![Security screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/security.png)

Tap **Security** to protect your account. Here you can:

- Change your password.
- Set up two-factor authentication, using an authenticator app or email. Turning it off needs a code of your second factor or a recovery code, which the app cannot send, so the app tells you to turn it off in the driver portal.
- Turn on **Require Face ID / fingerprint** so the app opens only with biometrics.

## Notifications

![Notification settings](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/notifications.png)

Tap **Notifications** to choose how the operator reaches you. Toggle email, SMS, and push notifications on or off. These control delivery; the bell in the header always keeps an in-app copy. See [Support](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/support) for the notification inbox.

## About

![About screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/about.png)

Tap **About** for the app and CSMS version numbers, the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links, and your operator contact details: address, email, and phone.

## Sign out

Tap **Sign Out** at the bottom of the Account hub to sign out of the app. You will need your email and password, plus any MFA code, to sign back in.
