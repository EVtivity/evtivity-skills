# Account and Settings

Manage your profile, security, notification channels, language, units, and legal information.

## Overview

The Account tab is the hub for everything about you: your profile, security, payment methods, RFID cards, vehicles, favorites, support, and app information. It shows your name, email, and the date you joined.

![Account hub](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/account.png)

## Personal info

![Personal info screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/personal.png)

Tap **Personal Info** to edit your first and last name and your phone number. Your email is shown but cannot be changed here. Below that, set your preferences:

- **Language** - the app updates immediately when you change it.
- **Distance unit** - miles or kilometers.
- **Timezone** - used for session and statement times.
- **Show prices** - **Including tax (gross)**, **Excluding tax (net)**, or the operator default. It sets whether charger prices and session costs are shown with or without tax. You pay the same amount either way. The choice is saved on your account, so the driver portal uses it too.

Save to apply your changes.

## Security

![Security screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/security.png)

Tap **Security** to protect your account. Here you can:

- Change your password.
- Set up or turn off two-factor authentication, using an authenticator app or email.
- Turn on **Require Face ID / fingerprint** so the app opens only with biometrics.

## Notifications

![Notification settings](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/notifications.png)

Tap **Notifications** to choose how the operator reaches you. Toggle email, SMS, and push notifications on or off. These control delivery; the bell in the header always keeps an in-app copy. See [Support](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/support) for the notification inbox.

## About

![About screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/about.png)

Tap **About** for the app and CSMS version numbers, the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links, and your operator contact details: address, email, and phone.

## Sign out

Tap **Sign Out** at the bottom of the Account hub to sign out of the app. You will need your email and password, plus any MFA code, to sign back in.
