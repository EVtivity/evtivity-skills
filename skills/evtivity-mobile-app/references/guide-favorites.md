# Favorites

Save the stations you use most and reach them in one tap.

## Overview

Favorites are the stations you charge at often. Save one and it is a tap away from the header on any main screen. You manage them under **Account &gt; Favorites**, or with the star on a station.

![Favorites list](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/favorites.png)

## Save a favorite

1. Open a station from search, nearby, or a QR scan.
2. Tap the star, or **Add to Favorites**, on the station detail.

The star at the top of the home and charge screens highlights in orange once you have at least one favorite.

## Open your favorites

Tap the star icon in the header, or open the **Account** tab and tap **Favorites**. Each entry shows the station name, address, whether it is online, and how many connectors are available. Tap one to go straight to its detail and start charging.

## Remove a favorite

On the Favorites list, tap the trash icon next to a station and confirm. You can also tap **Remove from Favorites** on the station detail.
