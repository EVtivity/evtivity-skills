# Station Watch

Get notified when a busy or offline station has a free connector.

## Overview

Station Watch tells you when a station frees up. When every connector is in use, or the station is offline, tap **Notify me when free** on its detail screen. The moment a connector becomes available, you get a notification through your usual channels (push, email, or SMS). It is one tap, and it clears itself once it fires.

![Watching list](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/watching.png)

## Watch a station

1. Open a station from search, nearby, or a QR scan.
2. If no connector is free, a **Notify me when free** button appears below the favorites button.
3. Tap it. The button changes to **Remove from Watching** to confirm. Tap it again to stop watching.

The **Notify me when free** button only shows when the station has nothing available right now. If a connector is already free, there is nothing to wait for, so the button is hidden and you can start charging.

## Get notified

When a connector at a watched station becomes available, EVtivity sends you a notification. It follows the same preferences as every other alert, which you set under **Account &gt; Notifications**. Tap the notification to jump straight to the station and start charging.

A watch is one-shot: it clears itself as soon as it notifies you. If you miss the window and the station fills up again, open it and tap **Notify me when free** to re-arm.

## Open your watches

Tap the eye icon in the header, to the right of the favorites star. The icon shows a count badge when you have active watches. It opens the **Watching** list, where each entry shows the station name, address, and current availability. Tap the trash icon to stop watching a station.

## Notes

- A watch expires on its own after 24 hours if the station never frees up.
- You can watch up to 25 stations at once.
- Starting a charge at a station you are watching clears that watch automatically.
- Watches are tied to your driver account and are separate from favorites.
