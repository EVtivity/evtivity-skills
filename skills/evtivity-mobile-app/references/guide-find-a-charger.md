# Find a Charger

Search for a station, find chargers near you, or scan a QR code on the charger.

## Overview

The Charge tab is where you find a place to charge. You can search by name or address, see chargers near your location, or scan the QR code printed on a charger. Each result leads to the station detail, where you pick a connector and start.

![Charge tab with a search result](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/charge-search.png)

## Search for a station

1. Tap the **Charge** tab.
2. Type a station name or address in the search box.
3. Tap a result to open the station detail.

Each result shows the station name, its site, how many connectors are available, and a status badge.

## See chargers near you

Under the search box is a **Nearby** section. The first time you use it, the app asks for permission to use your location. Allow it, and nearby stations appear automatically, closest first. If you do not grant location access, the Nearby list stays empty and you can still search by name.

## Scan a QR code

![QR scanner](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/qr-scan.png)

1. On the Charge tab, tap **Scan QR**.
2. Allow camera access the first time.
3. Point the camera at the QR code on the charger.

The app reads the code, opens that station, and preselects the connector printed on the label, so you can start charging in one more tap.

> **Tip:**
>
> Scanning is the fastest way to start at a station you are standing next to. The code points straight to the right connector, so you do not have to match port numbers by hand.

## What you see at a station

Opening a result takes you to the station detail: the address, the price, your default payment card, and a tile for each connector with its type, power, and live status. Prices are shown with or without tax as your **Show prices** choice in [Account and settings](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/account-and-settings) sets, with a note such as "Prices include 19% tax". From there you select an available connector and start. See [Start and stop a charge](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/start-and-stop-a-charge).
