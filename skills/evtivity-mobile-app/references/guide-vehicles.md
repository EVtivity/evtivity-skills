# Vehicles

Add your electric vehicles and assign them to sessions for accurate range estimates.

## Overview

Adding your vehicles lets the app estimate the range each session adds and feed the Distance trend on the Activity tab. You manage vehicles under **Account &gt; Vehicles**.

![Vehicles screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/vehicles.png)

## Add a vehicle

1. Open the **Account** tab and tap **Vehicles**.
2. Tap **Add Vehicle**.
3. Choose the make, then the model, from the lists.
4. Optionally enter the year.
5. Optionally choose the color and the body type, or leave **Not Set**.
6. Tap **Add**.

The vehicle appears in your list with its make, model, and year.

## Change the color or body type

Tap the pencil icon next to a vehicle, choose a color and a body type (**Sedan**, **Hatchback**, **SUV**, **Pickup Truck**, **Van**, **Box Truck**, **Bus** or **Semi Truck**), or **Not Set** to remove one, and tap **Save**. The list shows each vehicle's year, color and body type.

## Remove a vehicle

Tap the trash icon next to a vehicle and confirm. Removing a vehicle does not change past sessions it was already assigned to.

## Assign a vehicle to a session

When you open a charging session, you can assign one of your vehicles to it. The session then uses that vehicle efficiency to estimate the range added and the distance figures in your trends and statements. You can change the assigned vehicle on the session detail at any time.

> **Tip:**
>
> If you drive more than one EV, add them all. Assign the right one to each session so your range and distance numbers stay accurate.
