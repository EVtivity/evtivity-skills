# RFID Cards

Add a tap-to-charge RFID card and activate or deactivate it.

## Overview

An RFID card lets you start a charge by tapping the card on the station reader, without opening the app. You manage your cards under **Account &gt; RFID Cards**.

![RFID Cards screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/rfid-cards.png)

Each card is listed with its identifier masked except the last few characters, its standard (for example ISO14443), and an active toggle.

## Add a card

1. Open the **Account** tab and tap **RFID Cards**.
2. Tap **Add RFID Card**.
3. Enter the card identifier printed on the card.
4. Tap **Add**. The card appears in your list, ready to use.

## Activate or deactivate a card

Each card has a toggle:

- **Activate** turns the card on so it can start sessions.
- **Deactivate** turns it off, for example if you misplace it.

When you deactivate a card, the app asks you to confirm. The toggle then settles on the new state. You can reactivate the card later if you find it.

> **Tip:**
>
> Deactivate a lost card right away. A deactivated card cannot start a session, so no one else can charge on your account with it.
