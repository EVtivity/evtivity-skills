# Start and Stop a Charge

Pick a connector, start a session, watch it live, and stop when you are done.

## Overview

Once you have opened a station, you choose a connector and start charging. While the session runs, the app shows live power, energy, and cost. You stop from the same screen when you are finished.

![Station detail with connectors](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/station-detail.png)

## Start a session

1. Open the station from search, nearby, or a QR scan.
2. Check that a payment card is shown. Tap **Change** to pick a different one if needed.
3. Plug the cable into your vehicle.
4. Select an **Available** connector tile. Unavailable connectors are greyed out and cannot be selected.
5. Tap **Start Charging**.

The app checks that the cable is detected. If it is not, a message asks you to plug in and try again. When the station accepts the start, the app opens the live session screen.

> **Note:**
>
> You need a saved payment method before you can start, unless your operator runs a free network. Add one under [Payments](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/payments).

## When you already have a session

You can run only one charging session at a time. If a session is already in progress, opening any station replaces the connector list and Start Charging button with a notice.

![Station detail while a session is in progress](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/station-detail-busy.png)

Tap **View Active Session** to jump to the running session, where you can monitor or stop it. Once that session ends, the station detail shows the connectors and the Start Charging button again.

## Watch a session live

![Live charging session](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/session-detail.png)

The session screen updates while you charge. It shows:

- Current power in kilowatts and energy delivered in kilowatt-hours.
- Time elapsed and the running cost.
- Your vehicle battery estimate and the CO₂ you have avoided.
- Charts of power and energy over time.

The connector status animates while the session is active. You can assign a vehicle to the session to improve the range estimate, or open a support case if something looks wrong.

> **Note:**
>
> The live charts and the **Stop Charging** button appear only while a session is charging. After a session ends, the same screen shows the final totals and the receipt.

## Stop a session

1. Open the active session from the home screen or the Activity tab.
2. Tap **Stop Charging**.
3. Confirm with **Stop Charging** in the dialog.

The station ends the session and the screen switches to the final summary: total energy, duration, cost, and CO₂ avoided.

## After charging

A completed session stays in your history. Open it any time from [Activity](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/activity-and-statements) to see the breakdown, the site and address, the vehicle used, and the payment status. Costs include tax. With **Show prices** set to excluding tax, the breakdown shows the net amount, the tax, and the total. Set to including tax, it shows the total and the tax it contains. If you left the car plugged in after charging finished, an idle warning reminds you to unplug so others can use the connector.
