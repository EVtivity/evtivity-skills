# Getting Started

Create an account, sign in, recover your password, and secure the app with MFA and biometrics.

## Overview

Before you can charge, you need an account with your charging operator. You can create one in the app, then sign in. This page covers sign-up, sign-in, password recovery, multi-factor authentication, and unlocking the app with Face ID or a fingerprint.

![Create account screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/register.png)

## Create an account

1. Open the app and tap **Create One** on the sign-in screen.
2. Enter your first name, last name, email, and a password.
3. Tap **Create Account**.
4. Check your inbox for a verification email and confirm your address.

> **Note:**
>
> Some operators create your account for you (for example, a fleet or workplace). In that case you receive an invite or a temporary password and only need to sign in.

## Sign in

![Sign in screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/login.png)

1. Enter the email and password for your account.
2. Tap **Sign In**.
3. If your account uses multi-factor authentication, enter the one-time code on the next screen.

If your details are wrong, the app shows an error and you can try again. Tap the eye icon in the password field to check what you typed.

## Recover your password

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot Password?**.
2. Enter your email and submit.
3. Open the reset link in the email and set a new password.
4. Return to the app and sign in with the new password.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds a second step at sign-in. You set it up in **Account &gt; Security**, where you can choose an authenticator app (TOTP), email, or SMS. After it is on, every sign-in asks for a one-time code in addition to your password. See [Account and settings](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/account-and-settings).

## Unlock with Face ID or fingerprint

If your device supports it, you can lock the app behind your face or fingerprint so it stays private even when your phone is unlocked. Turn this on in **Account &gt; Security**. After that, the app asks for biometric confirmation each time you open it.
