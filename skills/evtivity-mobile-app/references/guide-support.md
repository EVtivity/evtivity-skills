# Support

Read your notifications and open a support case to chat with the operator.

## Overview

When you need help, the app lets you open a support case and chat with the operator in real time. The bell in the header keeps you up to date on sessions, reservations, and replies to your cases.

![Notifications inbox](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/notifications-inbox.png)

## Notifications

Tap the bell at the top of the home, charge, or account screen to open your notifications. They cover session receipts, when a charge starts and completes, reservation changes, and replies on your support cases. Opening the inbox marks the notifications as read and clears the badge. You choose which channels you receive (push, email, SMS) under [Account and settings](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/account-and-settings).

## Open a support case

![New support case](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/support-new.png)

1. Open the **Account** tab and tap **Support**, or tap the **Support** card on the home screen.
2. Tap **New Case**.
3. If the screen asks **How should we handle your request?**, choose first (see [Ask AI first](#ask-ai-first)).
4. Enter a subject, choose a category, and describe the problem.
5. Tap **Submit**. The case opens to its chat thread.

You can also reach support from a charging session, which links the case to that session so the operator has the details.

## Ask AI first

When the operator offers it, a new case starts with a choice: let an AI analyze your issue first, or send the case straight to the support team. You choose for each case. The option shows only when the operator turned it on.

![Choose how to handle your request](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/support-ai-consent.png)

1. Tap **New Case**. The screen asks **How should we handle your request?** and shows **About the AI analysis**:
   - An AI gives an automatic first answer. It is not a promise, for example of a refund.
   - Your request and the data listed below are sent to an external AI provider.
   - You can still reach a person: answer No or write a message, and the support team takes over.
   - You decide for each request. If you send it to the support team, nothing is sent to the AI.
2. Open **What the AI reads** to see the data the AI uses: this request, your last 10 charging sessions of the past 30 days, your charging cards and tokens (last 4 characters only), your payments and reservations of the past 30 days, and the stations you used.
3. Tap **Ask AI first** or **Send to the team**. The two buttons are equal and nothing is selected for you. The form opens only after you choose.
4. Fill in the case and tap **Submit**.

The form shows your choice at the top. Tap **Change** to choose again before you submit. The notice also links to the operator's **Privacy policy**.

> **Note:**
>
> **What the AI receives**
>
> The AI reads your request and your own charging sessions, payments, reservations and the stations you used. Stored credentials, such as passwords, are never sent. Card numbers and IBANs are cut to their last 4 characters. Email addresses and phone numbers are masked. Charging card and token ids are shortened to their last 4 characters.

With **Send to the team**, the case goes to the support team at once and nothing is sent to the AI.

### The automatic answer

![Automatic answer](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/support-ai-answer.png)

With **Ask AI first**, the case shows **Analyzing your issue** while the AI works. The app checks the case every few seconds while it is open, so the answer appears without a refresh. You also get a notification through the channels you chose.

The answer appears in the chat as **Automatic answer**, sometimes with suggested steps such as **Try another connector**. The AI gives one answer per case. It does not hold a conversation. Below the answer, the case asks **Did this solve your issue?**:

- **Yes**: the case is resolved and shows **Resolved. You can reopen it by writing to us.**
- **No**: the case goes to the support team and shows **Sent to the support team.**

The support team also takes over when you write a message before you answer, when you do not answer within the time the operator set (24 hours by default), or when the AI cannot answer, for example because of a provider error or a usage limit. The case then shows **We sent your request to the support team.** A case the AI resolved opens again and goes to the support team when you write a message on it.

## Chat on a case

![Support case chat](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/support-chat.png)

A case is a chat thread. Your messages appear on the right, the operator replies on the left, and grey system messages mark status changes. Type in the box at the bottom and send. You keep replying until the case is resolved.

The case header shows the case number, the category, and a status badge so you always know where it stands. Open any case again from the Support list, where cases are sorted with the most recently updated on top.

> **Tip:**
>
> Open a case from the charging session that had the problem. The operator sees the station, connector, and time without you having to describe them.
