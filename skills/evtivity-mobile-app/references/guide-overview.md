# End User Guide

How drivers use the EVtivity mobile app to find chargers, start sessions, pay, and manage their account.

This guide is for drivers. It walks through every part of the EVtivity mobile app, from creating an account to charging, paying, reserving a connector, and getting support. Each page covers one area of the app with the screens you will actually see.

![EVtivity mobile app home screen](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/home.png)

## What the app does

The app is your charging account in your pocket. With it you can:

- Find a charger by search, by nearby location, or by scanning a QR code on the station.
- Start and stop a charging session and watch it live.
- Pay with a saved card.
- Reserve a connector for later.
- Review past sessions, monthly statements, and the CO₂ you avoided.
- Manage vehicles, RFID cards, favorite stations, and notifications.
- Open a support case and chat with the operator.

## Getting the app

Download the app from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). The exact name and icon depend on your charging network, because each operator publishes the app under its own brand. Open it and sign in, or create an account if you are new. See [Getting started](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/getting-started).

## Finding your way around

The home screen greets you by name and shows your active session, your recent sessions, and quick links to Payment Methods, RFID Cards, Vehicles, and Support. The star and bell at the top open your favorite stations and your notifications.

The bottom of every screen has five tabs:

1. **Home** - your dashboard and quick actions.
2. **Charge** - find a charger and start a session.
3. **Activity** - your charging history and statements.
4. **Reserve** - book a connector for later.
5. **Account** - your profile, payment methods, and settings.

> **Note:**
>
> Some features depend on how your operator has set up their network. Reservations, roaming, and payments can be turned on or off, so a screen described here may not appear if your operator has disabled it.
