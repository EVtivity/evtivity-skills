# Activity and Statements

Review past sessions, see your charging trends, and open monthly statements.

## Overview

The Activity tab is your charging history. It shows every session by month, a trend chart of your cost, energy, or distance, and the CO₂ you avoided. From here you also open a monthly statement you can keep for your records.

![Activity tab with the trend chart](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/activity.png)

## Browse your sessions

1. Tap the **Activity** tab.
2. Use the arrows next to the month to move between months.
3. Scroll the Sessions list to see each session for that month.
4. Tap a session to open its full detail.

Each row shows the station, the date, the energy delivered, and the cost, followed by "incl. tax" when the session has tax.

![Sessions list for the month](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/activity-sessions.png)

## Read your trends

Above the list is a chart for the trailing six months. Use the toggle to switch what it measures:

- **Cost** - what you spent.
- **Energy** - kilowatt-hours delivered.
- **Distance** - estimated range added, based on your vehicles.

The selected month is highlighted. Tap any bar to jump to that month. Below the chart, a line shows the CO₂ you avoided for the month.

## Open a monthly statement

![Monthly statement](https://www.evtivity.com/screenshots/mobile/statement.png)

1. On the Activity tab, tap **Monthly statement**.
2. Review the itemized list: each session with its date, location, energy, cost, and CO₂. The cost reads **Cost (incl. tax)** when a session cost includes tax.
3. The totals card at the top sums the session count, energy, cost, and CO₂ for the month.

Use the statement for expense reports or to reconcile your charging spend.

> **Tip:**
>
> Assign a vehicle to your sessions so the Distance trend and the range estimates are accurate. You manage vehicles under [Vehicles](https://www.evtivity.com/docs/mobile-app/guide/vehicles).
